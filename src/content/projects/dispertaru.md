Dispertaru is a web-based CRUD application for the submission and management of Perpanjangan Hak Guna Bangunan (HGB) requests under the Dinas Pertanahan dan Tata Ruang.

## A more organized request process

The application digitizes the request process, making submissions and their management easier to handle while reducing reliance on manual paperwork.
